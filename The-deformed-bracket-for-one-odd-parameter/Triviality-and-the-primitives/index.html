Theorem 4.5 (Triviality for one odd parameter). The maps T=\mathrm{id}+\kappa\tilde h_R and T^{-1}=\mathrm{id}-\kappa\tilde h_R are even, R-linear, mutually inverse, and satisfy

[TX,TY]_0=T([X,Y]_\beta)

for all X,Y\in\mathfrak g_R, where [\cdot,\cdot]_\beta is the bracket of Section 4.1.

Theorem 4.7. We have \Gamma_\beta=\delta f_\beta, and for all indices

f_\beta(L_{uv})=0,\qquad f_\beta(F_{w,j})=-\sum_t\check\beta_{t,j}L_{tw},\qquad f_\beta(S_{ij})=2\sum_t\bigl(\check\beta_{t,j}F_{t,i}-\check\beta_{t,i}F_{t,j}\bigr).

So f_\beta vanishes on \mathfrak{sp}(2n), maps the odd part into \mathfrak{sp}(2n) and maps \mathfrak{so}(N) into the odd part. For N=1, f_\beta is the primitive of [Aoi26, Theorem 4.1].
